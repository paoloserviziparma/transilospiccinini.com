Mobile vacuum unit · ItalyMobile vacuum unloading of plastic granules

Our electric vacuum equipment recovers plastic granules wherever a silo truck cannot be loaded the usual way.

Request a quote →

At a glance

  • Electric vacuum unit, compact and transportable
  • Up to about 5 tonnes an hour
  • Can work inside production areas
  • Loads several vehicles in sequence or fills big bags

Who it is for

Vacuum unloading solves cases where material cannot be loaded the usual way:

  • plants with a silo to empty because of contaminated material
  • sites not set up for silo truck loading
  • warehouses with damaged big bags that can no longer be moved
  • swap bodies or silo trailers involved in an accident

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How much material can you vacuum per hour?

Up to about 5 tonnes an hour.

Can you work inside the plant?

Yes, the equipment is electric and compact and can be taken inside production areas.

Do you handle vehicles after an accident?

Yes, we empty swap bodies and silo trailers involved in accidents.

Do you come to our plant?

Yes, the equipment is transportable and works at the customer’s site, including inside production areas.

Where does the vacuumed material go?

We load it onto one or more vehicles or pack it into big bags on site.
