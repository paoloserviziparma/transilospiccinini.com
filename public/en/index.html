About us

Over 100 years in transport, 50 in bulk.

We specialise in the transport, discharge, storage and repackaging of plastic granules and mineral powders. Our fleet includes pressurised tipping silo trailers and tipping container chassis for 20', 30' and 40' boxes fitted with a rotary valve.

  1. 1975Specialisation in bulk transport with tipping silo trailers and container chassis with rotary valve.
  2. 1997Opening of the depot at the Parma inland terminal, with intermodal rail link.
  3. 2014New head office: management, traffic, depot, accounts and workshop under one roof.
  4. 2023Automated bag-cutting machine for repackaging.

Frequently asked questions

Before you call

Do you issue a cleaning certificate?

Yes. After every trip we thoroughly clean the silo and fittings and issue a cleaning and fitness-to-load certificate, which can travel with the transport documents on request.

What materials do you carry?

Plastic granules and mineral powders, in bulk in pressurised tipping silo trailers or in lined containers and swap bodies.

Which containers do you handle?

We carry and discharge 20', 30' and 40' containers; at our depot we also transload 45' boxes.

Can I use my own packaging for repackaging?

Yes, we use your packaging or supply it ourselves: 25 kg bags, big bags from 500 to 1,200 kg, octabins and bulk.

Where are you based?

At Via Parigi 8, Interporto di Parma, Bianconese, 43010 Fontevivo (PR), Italy. The depot is linked to the inland terminal’s railway container yard.

Do you work outside Italy?

We work in the Italian market, with a particular focus on intermodal traffic and goods arriving at ports.
