Bulk haulage · Parma, ItalySilo truck and container transport

Our pressurised tipping silo trailers carry granular and powdered products, mainly plastics and mineral powders. For containers and swap bodies we use dedicated chassis reserved for discharging plastic granules.

Request a quote →

At a glance

  • Pressurised tipping silo trailers of 56, 60 and 62 m³ in aluminium alloy
  • Pneumatic discharge at 1 bar with micron filter and intercooler
  • Container chassis with rotary valve for 20', 30' and 40' boxes
  • Cleaning after every trip with a fitness-to-load certificate

Who it is for

The service is designed for anyone who needs bulk plastic granules or mineral powders delivered straight into a plant’s silo:

  • polymer producers and distributors
  • injection moulding, extrusion and compounding companies with storage silos
  • freight forwarders and logistics operators with goods arriving at ports or by rail
  • industries using mineral powders

Two set-ups, one goal: a clean discharge

Container chassis

  • 20', 30' and 40' containers
  • Tipping trailers up to 40°
  • Rotary (star) valve
  • Dry, low-noise compressors, cooled according to the product
  • Reserved for plastic granules only

Containers we discharge

  • Containers with liner
  • Swap bodies with liner
  • Hopper containers with gravity discharge
  • Bulk containers with pressure discharge

How we work

  1. Loading and sealingThe silo is loaded through the top hatches and closed with a TIR cable for hermetic sealing.
  2. Pneumatic dischargeTipping up to 40° and discharge at 1 bar: the air goes through a micron filter and is cooled by the intercooler.
  3. Cleaning and certificateAfter every trip we thoroughly clean silo, hoses, bends and fittings and issue a cleaning and fitness-to-load certificate.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

What do you carry?

Granular and powdered products, mainly plastics and mineral powders, with pressurised tipping silo trailers and container chassis.

Do you issue a cleaning certificate?

Yes. For every trip we issue a cleaning and fitness-to-load certificate, which can travel with the transport documents if the customer asks.

What capacity are your silo trailers?

56, 60 or 62 m³, with an aluminium alloy body.

Can you discharge lined containers?

Yes: lined containers and swap bodies, hopper containers with gravity discharge and bulk containers with pressure discharge, in 20', 30' and 40' sizes.

Where do you operate?

We work in the Italian market, with a particular focus on intermodal traffic and goods arriving at ports.

How much does a silo trailer carry?

Our silo trailers have a volume of 56, 60 or 62 m³. The payload depends on the product’s density and on Italian road weight limits.

Why the micron filter and intercooler?

The micron filter cleans the air that touches the product during discharge; the intercooler cools it so the granules are not heated.
