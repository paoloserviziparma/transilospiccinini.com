Repackaging · Parma, ItalyRepackaging: from bags to bulk and back

Our automated lines empty and fill material in several formats: you can buy in the most convenient format and let us convert it into the one you need.

Request a quote →

At a glance

  • Automated cutting of 25 kg bags, since 2023
  • Emptying and filling of big bags (500–1,200 kg), octabins, silo trucks and containers
  • Your packaging or ours
  • Bulk or packed delivery with our own vehicles

Who it is for

Repackaging pays off when the format you buy in is not the format you use:

  • processors who receive bags but feed from silos
  • plants without silos that need big bags or octabins
  • distributors repacking lots for different customers
  • anyone receiving containers who wants the material packed

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own packaging?

Yes, we use the customer’s packaging or supply it ourselves.

Which big bags do you handle?

Big bags from 500 to 1,200 kg, in several formats.

Since when do you offer repackaging?

Since 2023, with an automated bag-cutting machine for loading tankers and filling big bags and octabins.

Can you deliver after repackaging?

Yes: we deliver in bulk with our silo trucks or packed in big bags and octabins.

Can you empty a container straight into big bags?

Yes, big bags and octabins can also be filled from silo trucks and containers.
