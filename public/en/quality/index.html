Certifications

Among the first certified companies in Italy

since 1993UNI EN ISO 9001:2015

Certified in 1993 by Certichim, later Certitrans and now Certiquality, under number 046. Renewed over the years and extended in 2000 to bulk plastics storage.

since 2008SQAS attestation

Assesses not only service efficiency but also attention to safety and sustainability.

since 2010SSA code of practice

Attestation in line with Italian Law 286, for all-round safety.

Safety

Quality also means

Trained people, protected vehicles, secure goods.

  • Ongoing staff training
  • Up-to-date personal protective equipment
  • Strict compliance with current regulations
  • Care for the integrity of goods, with anti-theft devices, trackers and constant vehicle monitoring
  • CCTV in yards and depots, with unhitched trailers protected by mechanical anti-theft devices

Our most important recognition, though, is our customers’ trust, which over the years has grown into solid, long-lasting partnerships.

Environment

Sustainability is part of the job

  • In 2014 we were awarded a 5-star Eco Stars rating, a European emissions-reduction scheme joined by the city of Parma.
  • Through our partnership with Michelin we obtained certification for sustainable tyre management, including regrooved and retreaded “Remix” tyres. According to Michelin’s estimates, in 2025 this saved up to 27.8 tonnes of CO₂ and 11.1 tonnes of raw materials.
  • In 2026 we installed a 72 kWp solar plant on our head office roof, with two storage batteries totalling 80 kWh, to increase energy independence and reduce our environmental impact. Project sheet (PDF, Italian)

Project carried out with the support of the European Union under the NextGenerationEU programme and Italy’s National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR). Project amount: €102,253.89 · Public grant awarded: €37,009.36.
