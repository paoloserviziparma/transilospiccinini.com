Silo trailer washing · Parma, ItalySilo trailer interior washing and certified weighbridge

At our site in the Parma inland terminal we run a washing station for the interior cleaning of silo trailers and a certified weighbridge.

Request a quote →

At a glance

  • Twin bays for interior washing of silo trailers
  • Twin fall-arrest lines and twin pressure-washer systems
  • Treatment and disposal of waste water
  • Hot-air drying and certified weighbridge

Why it matters

A clean, dry silo prevents residues from the previous load contaminating the next one. That is why after every trip we clean silo, hoses, bends and fittings and issue a cleaning and fitness-to-load certificate.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you only wash your own vehicles?

No, the washing station is reserved for our own silo trailers, so every silo that arrives for loading has been cleaned to our procedures.

Is the weighbridge certified?

Yes, it is a 60-tonne certified and raised weighbridge.

How is the silo dried after washing?

With hot air, so it is ready for the next load.

What happens to the washing water?

Waste water is collected, treated and disposed of.
